# Brand files

Every SVG and every icon PNG here is generated by
`scripts/gen_app_icon.py` (`./build.sh gen-icon`); the lockup PNGs by
`scripts/render_lockup.py`. Edit the scripts, not the files;
`./build.sh gen-icon --check` is a CI gate.

The mark is the cube: the E and the S projected onto the two front
walls of an isometric cube 58 units tall, the E on the left wall in
the ink and the S on the right wall in the accent, each a 5x5
dot-matrix letter drawn in the plane of its wall (lit cells only), and
a 5x5 field of unlit cells in `#64636A`, one fixed grey in both
colour sets, on the top face. Each
face's grid fills it to a 4% margin.

`app/lib/widgets/eclipsa_studio_mark.dart` draws the same geometry on screen;
`app/test/eclipsa_studio_mark_asset_test.dart` checks the painter against
`eclipsa-studio-mark.svg`.

Two colour sets, one per ground:

| Set | Plate | E (ink) | S (accent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| dark (the app icon on macOS and Windows): Dark's bgBase, textPrimary and pink, read from `app/design/tokens/dark.tokens.json` | `#101010` | `#E3E1F2` (14.79:1) | `#E06090` (5.66:1) |
| light (a light ground: a page, a slide) | `#F5F3EE` | `#18181B` (15.98:1) | `#BE185D` (5.44:1) |

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `eclipsa-studio-mark.svg`, `eclipsa-studio-mark-light.svg` | the mark alone on a transparent ground, in its 58-unit box |
| `eclipsa-studio-app-icon.svg`, `eclipsa-studio-app-icon-light.svg` | the icon board: a 1024 board with the 824 plate at a 22.5% corner and the cube's silhouette 72% of it tall, centred |
| `eclipsa-studio-lockup.svg`, `eclipsa-studio-lockup-light.svg` | the cube beside the wordmark on the plate; the wordmark is SVG text in Inter at weight 600, "Eclipsa" in the ink and " Studio" in the accent, 30 units of type to the mark's 58-unit box (the app draws 0.515x) with -2% letter spacing, cap height centred on the cube, 20 units from its silhouette |
| `png/eclipsa-studio-app-icon-{256,1024}.png`, `png/eclipsa-studio-app-icon-light-{256,1024}.png` | the icon board rasterised |
| `png/eclipsa-studio-lockup.png`, `png/eclipsa-studio-lockup-light.png`, `png/eclipsa-studio-lockup-stacked.png` | the lockup rasterised at 3 px per unit, one per set and the two stacked; set in the bundled Inter, so these do not depend on installed fonts |

The macOS icon is generated twice, both in the dark set.
`app/macos/Runner/AppIcon.icon` is an Icon Composer document
(`icon.json` plus one SVG per layer under `Assets/`: the E, the S and
the top-face field), which macOS 26 draws with the system's glass
treatment on the dots, and from the layers' shapes for the tinted and
clear looks. Its fills carry no per-appearance specialization, so
Light mode and Dark mode show the same dark plate. The field layer's
fill is the same fixed grey.
`app/macos/Runner/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` is the PNG set.
With both in the project, actool writes the bundle's `AppIcon.icns`
from the Icon Composer document, which is what macOS before 26 shows.
The Windows `app_icon.ico` is the dark set on a plate that fills the
square (18% corners) with the cube spanning 84% of it, so the cube stays
legible at the 16 to 32 px the taskbar and notification area use.

The lockup SVGs are set in Inter, the face the app bundles; a viewer
without Inter draws them in a substitute face and the text lands a
little short or long of the right margin. The PNG lockups are the
reference rendering.

## The Eclipsa Audio lockup

Eclipsa Audio is the format's consumer brand, a different mark from
the app's cube. The app draws its horizontal greyscale lockup on the
landing screen (`app/lib/widgets/eclipsa_audio_lockup.dart`), and none
of the logo suite is checked in. What the repo carries is:

- the symbol as geometry: each half is an outer and a tall inner shape,
  each two cubic Bézier curves mirrored about the horizontal axis, in a
  356 x 324 box, with the overlap filled in the core colour;
- the wordmark as twelve Google Sans glyph outlines
  (`eclipsa_audio_wordmark_data.dart`, in font units, written by `scripts/gen_eclipsa_audio_wordmark.py` from the OFL font
  in google/fonts), placed where the brand's lockup has them;
- the six palette colours (`app/lib/eclipsa_audio_palette.dart`):
  black, Grey 700 `#5F6368`, Grey 600 `#80868B`, Grey 500 `#9AA0A6`,
  Grey 400 `#BDC1C6`, white.

The positive colourway (Grey 400 and Grey 500 shapes, black core and
wordmark) is for light grounds and the reverse (Grey 600 and Grey 500,
white core and wordmark) for dark ones; the widget picks by the
ground's luminance.
